# City and character polish v0.5.0

User intent: improve the existing mobile destruction loop with richer reusable city assets, convincing damage and collapses, reliable rooftop actions, expressive trials of real character rigs, more physical throws and an unobstructed impact sightline.

Implemented work:
1. Reuse 3D Maker cars and street props, add gardens and streamed cleanup.
2. Accumulate section cracks; propagate lost structural support into roof/floor quadrants; keep all building variants destructible.
3. Sweep descending feet onto real roof/floor support; belly-flop then stand on slam.
4. Add articulated flight and inertial car wrecks while preserving swept impacts and wall splats.
5. Trial rigged Godzilla/Kong alongside the creator, compose distinct perks and bind actual hands/mouth.
6. Move routine feedback to the screen edge, keep the target area clear.

Acceptance evidence: see docs/test-build-verification.md v0.5.0. Phone visual and performance checks remain explicitly open; provenance and authored/procedural animation distinctions are in docs/monster-assets.md.
